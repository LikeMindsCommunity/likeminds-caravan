Relevant Communities. Structured conversations. Meaningful relationship

Make your niche community discoverable and add relevant authenticated members

Simply list your niche community on CollabMates to make it shareable and discoverable on the internet. Members sign in with their social media profiles and provide additional information required by you to authenticate them before getting access to the private secured link to join the community.

Reduce spam by allowing parallel conversations in your growing community

Instead of chatting in a single window, members can now chat parallely on conversation cards. These are simple cards that support chat, are followable and shareable across platforms. Imagine WhatsApp with threaded chats and instead of getting notifications on all the messages, you get notified for only relevant ones!

Drive meaningful relationships between members and develop belongingness

We link community members’ profile with their social media profiles so that everyone knows each other better. Meaningful interactions with known people in a purpose-driven community create a feeling of belongingness in the members. Build a strong trustworthy community with real identities!
